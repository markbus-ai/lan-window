# LAN Window

Inferred from the project's brief and the owner's requests; no separate product interview was run.

## Product

LAN Window sends a Linux PC's screen to a window on a Windows PC over a trusted local network, so that window can be shared in Discord. Video only, no audio.

## Users and scene

One person at home with two PCs, usually while gaming or streaming. They set it up once per session and then look at it only when something is wrong. The owner hands the packaged executables to a friend who has no build tools.

## Register

Operate. Success is knowing the connection state at a glance and starting or stopping it without reading.

## Commitments

- UI copy is in Spanish.
- Dark and sober, chosen by the owner after rejecting a brutalist version: colour marks state only.
- The video surface carries no chrome, addresses, or status text once video is live, because Discord captures it.
- Trusted LAN only: no encryption or authentication, and no claim otherwise.
